How it works

Four steps from a messy idea list to a ranked list you can defend.

List your ideasPaste them in. Add a line on each and the keywords people would search.
Set your DialsPick the factors that matter to you and how much each one counts.
Pull real dataSearch demand, competition, your own domains and assets. Gaps show as "no data".
Convene the councilAI models judge blind, debate, and land on one score with a confidence level.

Dials: your factors, your weights

A Dial is one thing you care about, turned up or down. Add, remove or reweight Dials any time and the ranking updates. The AI models never see your weights, so they judge the idea, not your preferences.

Real data first

Monthly searches and competition come from Google data providers. Asset fit checks the domains you already own. Each score links to its source.

Honest about gaps

No data means "no data", never a guess. Ideas with thin evidence are pulled toward the middle so one lucky number can't win.

Your numbers count too

Type in market size, cost to build or weeks to revenue, with where you got it. Your notes travel with the score.

The council: blind first, then a fair debate

Research on AI debate shows independent answers are a strong baseline and that long debates add little. So the council is built to stay honest.

1. Blind round

Each model scores the idea alone, with its reasons and risks. This round is kept and shown, so you can see if the debate changed anything.

2. Anonymous debate

Models see each other only as "Panelist A, B, C". A moderator frames the disagreements, one panelist plays devil's advocate, and scores change only for a stated reason.

3. Forced consensus

The final score is the panel's median, worked out by the software, not picked by a chairman. You get a confidence level and the strongest dissent in plain English.

Questions

How does IdeaConclave rank ideas?

Each idea gets a 0–10 score on every Dial that has data. Your weights combine them into one score. Ideas missing a lot of data are pulled toward a neutral middle, and a coverage badge shows how much of the score rests on real data.

Which AI models sit on the council?

Claude, GPT and Gemini to start, and any other model with a compatible API. Using different companies' models is on purpose: they make different mistakes.

Who pays for the AI calls?

You bring your own API keys. IdeaConclave shows an estimated cost before every run and stops at a spending cap you set.

Will it tell me my idea is great?

Only if the panel thinks so. Models are told to be kind but honest, to treat missing data as unknown, and not to invent numbers. The strongest dissent is always shown.
